Most people have only a vague idea where their money goes. They earn X, spend Y, and wonder why there's nothing left at the end of the month. A monthly budget review fixes this.

## Awareness Before Optimization

You can't improve what you don't measure. The first benefit of a monthly review isn't optimization—it's simply knowing. Where did the money actually go? Not where you think it went, where it *actually* went.

This awareness alone often changes behavior. When you see $150/month on subscription services you barely use, or $400 on restaurant delivery, you don't need a budget to tell you to cut back. The data speaks for itself.

## The Compounding Effect

A monthly budget review compounds in three ways:

**Financial**: Small leaks identified and fixed add up to thousands per year
**Behavioral**: Regular reviews create accountability and mindfulness
**Strategic**: Tracking progress toward goals maintains momentum

Someone who reviews their finances monthly is infinitely more likely to achieve financial goals than someone who "sets and forgets" a budget.

## Beyond Budgeting

While "budget review" is in the name, this habit is really about financial awareness and intentionality. You're checking:
- Where money went (spending review)
- Progress toward goals (savings, debt payoff, net worth)
- Patterns over time (seasonality, lifestyle creep, improvements)
- Upcoming expenses (insurance, taxes, repairs)

It's your monthly financial health check-up.

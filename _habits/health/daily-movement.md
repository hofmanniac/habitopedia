time_investment: short
how_to_start: |
  Commit to just 10 minutes of movement daily. A walk around the block, gentle stretching, or a short YouTube workout video all count. Schedule it at the same time each day — many find success first thing in the morning or during lunch. Focus on consistency over intensity.
tips:
  - "Start small — even 5-10 minutes counts and builds the habit"
  - "Stack with existing habits (e.g., walk after breakfast)"
  - "Take the stairs instead of the elevator"
  - "Park farther away from entrances"
  - "Set a timer to stand and stretch every hour if you sit at a desk"
  - "Walk during phone calls when possible"
  - "Use a step counter or app to track and motivate yourself"
  - "Find movement you enjoy — dancing, gardening, playing with kids all count"
common_pitfalls:
  - "All-or-nothing thinking — 'I don't have time for a full workout' so doing nothing"
  - "Sitting for long stretches without breaks"
  - "Only counting 'formal exercise' — all movement matters"
  - "Being too sedentary on rest days"
  - "Ignoring the cumulative benefits of small movements throughout the day"
success_metrics:
  - "Moving at least 10-30 minutes daily"
  - "Reaching 7,000-10,000 steps per day"
  - "Improved energy levels"
  - "Better sleep quality"
  - "Reduced stiffness and joint pain"
  - "Improved mood and reduced anxiety"
  - "Maintaining consistency 6-7 days per week"

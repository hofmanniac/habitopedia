how_to_start: |
  Learn one simple technique: Box Breathing (4-4-4-4). Inhale for 4 counts, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4. Repeat 4-5 rounds. Practice this once daily in a calm moment, then deploy it when you notice stress rising. Even 2-3 minutes can shift your state.
tips:
  - "Start with just 5 minutes daily to build the habit"
  - "Practice when calm so you remember it when stressed"
  - "Set reminders to take 'breathing breaks' throughout the day"
  - "Use it before important meetings, presentations, or difficult conversations"
  - "Try different techniques: Box Breathing, 4-7-8, or Physiological Sigh"
  - "Breathe through your nose when possible for better nervous system activation"
  - "Focus on making exhales longer than inhales for maximum relaxation"
  - "Combine with a quick body scan to notice tension"
common_pitfalls:
  - "Only trying this when extremely stressed — practice regularly first"
  - "Breathing too fast or too deeply (causing lightheadedness)"
  - "Expecting instant magic — benefits compound over time"
  - "Forgetting to use it when you actually need it"
  - "Dismissing it as 'too simple' without trying it consistently"
  - "Mouth breathing instead of nose breathing"

  - "Using breathing automatically when stress arises"

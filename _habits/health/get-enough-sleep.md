how_to_start: |
  Calculate your ideal bedtime by counting back 8 hours from when you need to wake up. Set a "bedtime alarm" 30 minutes before that time. When it goes off, start your wind-down routine: dim lights, put away screens, and prepare for bed. Do this consistently for 2 weeks to establish the pattern.
tips:
  - "Keep your bedroom cool (65-68°F / 18-20°C) for optimal sleep"
  - "Make your room as dark as possible — use blackout curtains or an eye mask"
  - "Avoid caffeine after 2pm to prevent sleep disruption"
  - "Stop eating 2-3 hours before bedtime"
  - "Expose yourself to morning sunlight to reinforce your circadian rhythm"
  - "Keep a consistent sleep schedule — even on weekends"
  - "Use your bed only for sleep (not work or TV)"
common_pitfalls:
  - "Inconsistent sleep schedule — going to bed at wildly different times"
  - "Screen time right before bed — blue light suppresses melatonin"
  - "Trying to 'catch up' on weekends — this disrupts circadian rhythm"
  - "Drinking alcohol to sleep — it reduces sleep quality"
  - "Exercising intensely within 2-3 hours of bedtime"
  - "Keeping the room too warm"
  - "Using sleep as a flex — prioritize it like any other health habit"
success_metrics:
  - "Waking up feeling refreshed without an alarm"

  - "Not needing caffeine to function in the morning"
  - "Improved mood and emotional regulation"
  - "Better focus and cognitive performance during the day"
  - "Consistent energy throughout the day"
  - "Tracking 7-9 hours of sleep time consistently"

how_to_start: |
  Start your day by drinking a full glass of water upon waking. Keep a reusable water bottle with you throughout the day. Aim for half your body weight in ounces (e.g., 150 lbs = 75 oz). Use triggers like "before each meal" or "every hour" to build the habit.
tips:
  - "Drink a glass of water first thing in the morning before coffee"
  - "Keep water visible — a bottle on your desk makes it easier"
  - "Use a marked water bottle to track intake throughout the day"
  - "Drink a glass before each meal (helps with digestion and portion control)"
  - "If you forget, set hourly reminders on your phone"
  - "Add lemon, cucumber, or fruit if plain water is unappealing"
  - "Increase intake when exercising, in hot weather, or at high altitude"
common_pitfalls:
  - "Waiting until you're thirsty — thirst means you're already dehydrated"
  - "Only drinking when convenient — make it a scheduled habit"
  - "Replacing water with coffee, soda, or juice — these don't hydrate as well"
  - "Chugging large amounts at once — spread intake throughout the day"
  - "Not adjusting for exercise or climate"

  - "Fewer headaches"

  - "Meeting your daily ounce target consistently"

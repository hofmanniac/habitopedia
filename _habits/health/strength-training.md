how_to_start: |
  Start with 2 sessions per week of 20-30 minutes. Bodyweight exercises (pushups, squats, lunges, planks) require no equipment. Focus on learning proper form before adding weight. Many beginners succeed with simple programs like "Starting Strength" or following along with beginner YouTube workouts.
tips:
  - "Consistency beats intensity — 2x per week is infinitely better than zero"
  - "Start with bodyweight exercises before adding weights"
  - "Focus on compound movements (squats, deadlifts, presses, rows)"
  - "Learn proper form first — consider hiring a trainer for initial sessions"
  - "Progressive overload: gradually increase weight, reps, or difficulty over time"
  - "Allow 48 hours between working the same muscle groups"
  - "Track your workouts to see progress and stay motivated"
  - "Prioritize this like a doctor's appointment — schedule it and show up"
common_pitfalls:
  - "Skipping strength training because cardio 'feels' more productive"
  - "Doing the same routine forever without progression"
  - "Poor form leading to injury"
  - "Not eating enough protein to support muscle growth"
  - "Training too frequently without adequate recovery"
  - "Comparing yourself to others instead of your past self"
  - "Quitting when you don't see immediate results — muscle builds slowly"
success_metrics:
  - "Completing 2-4 strength sessions per week consistently"
  - "Progressive increase in weight or reps over time"
  - "Improved functional strength (carrying groceries, lifting objects)"
  - "Better posture and reduced back pain"
  - "Increased muscle definition"
  - "Improved bone density (verified by DEXA scan)"
  - "Better body composition (more muscle, less fat)"
  - "Feeling stronger in daily activities"

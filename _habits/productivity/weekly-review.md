The weekly review is your pause button. In a world of constant doing, it's your dedicated time to step back, look at the bigger picture, and course-correct before small misalignments become major problems.

## The Compounding Effect

Without a weekly review, you're like a ship without a compass check. Each day you sail in roughly the right direction, but small navigation errors compound. After months, you look up and realize you're nowhere near your intended destination.

With a weekly review, you course-correct every seven days. You catch scope creep on projects, notice energy drains, identify patterns in how you spend time, and realign your daily actions with your larger goals.

## What Makes It Powerful

The weekly review creates three essential outcomes:

**1. Closure**: You empty your mental cache of open loops, incomplete tasks, and nagging reminders. This cognitive offload creates mental space for deeper thinking.

**2. Perspective**: Zooming out from daily urgencies, you see patterns: which activities energized you, which drained you, what moved the needle, what was busy-work.

**3. Intention**: Rather than letting next week happen to you, you decide what you want to accomplish and create the conditions to make it likely.

It's the difference between living reactively week after week versus designing your life deliberately.

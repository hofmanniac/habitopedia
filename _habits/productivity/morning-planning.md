Morning planning is the habit of deciding what matters most before the day decides for you. Without it, you're in reactive mode from the moment you wake up: responding to emails, handling urgencies, fighting fires. With it, you start each day with clarity about what you're trying to accomplish and how you'll make it happen.

## The Planning Paradox

Many people resist planning because it feels like time away from "real work." But here's the paradox: 10 minutes of planning saves hours of wasted effort. When you don't plan:

- You work on whatever seems urgent (often not important)
- You jump between tasks, fragmenting focus
- You spend mental energy deciding "what should I work on next?"
- You end the day having been busy but not productive

When you do plan, you work with intention. You've already decided what matters most, blocked time for it, and created the conditions for deep focus.

## The Daily Question

Morning planning answers one essential question: **"What does done look like today?"**

Not done for the week, month, or year—done for TODAY. What are the 1-3 things that, if accomplished, would make this a successful day? Everything else is secondary.
